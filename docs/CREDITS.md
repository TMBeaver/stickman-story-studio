# Google Flow credits and production budgets

**Checked: November 2, 2026.** These are planning notes based on the official pages below and one observed generation quote. Google can change plans, model access and prices. The live quote in the intended account is authoritative before spending. Local subscription and top-up prices must be checked at checkout; no specific euro price is assumed here.

## Allocations

The current Flow credit page lists 50 daily credits with or without a subscription, plus monthly credits for subscribers:

| Plan | Additional monthly Flow credits |
| --- | ---: |
| No subscription | 0 |
| Google AI Plus | 200 |
| Google AI Pro | 1,000 |
| Google AI Ultra $100 tier (Google's page label) | 10,000 |
| Google AI Ultra $200 tier (Google's page label) | 25,000 |

The dollar-tier labels identify the published tiers, not a verified Lithuanian checkout price. Daily unused credits do not accumulate; monthly unused credits do not carry into the next billing cycle. Free video access may be constrained at peak demand.

Our account displayed 50 free credits. Flow quoted 15 for the approved ten-second clip; no paid subscription was purchased for that test.

## Current Omni 720p generation costs

| Generated duration | Credits per output |
| --- | ---: |
| 4 seconds | 7 |
| 6 seconds | 10 |
| 8 seconds | 12 |
| 10 seconds | 15 |

Some requests produce multiple outputs; the cost is per generated output. 360p draft generation is cheaper but does not establish the final 720p visual quality. Avoid changing models solely to meet a budget without checking whether the accepted style and audio quality hold.

## Longer-video estimates

The following assumes full coverage with ten-second 720p clips, one usable output per segment, no discarded handles, no extra reference-image generation and no additional edit/voice charges:

| Final target | Ten-second clips | One-take minimum | Suggested 2x-3x planning allowance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 30 seconds | 3 | 45 | 90-135 |
| 1 minute | 6 | 90 | 180-270 |
| 3 minutes | 18 | 270 | 540-810 |
| 5 minutes | 30 | 450 | 900-1,350 |
| 10 minutes | 60 | 900 | 1,800-2,700 |

These are arithmetic estimates, not guaranteed production yields. A 1,000-credit Pro allocation covers approximately 66 ten-second generations before any daily allocation, or eleven minutes of raw footage. Accepted edited runtime can be much shorter after retries, trimming and unused takes.

## Getting more credits

- Wait for the daily free allocation to refresh for low-volume experiments.
- Subscribe to Google AI Pro for a larger monthly allowance and better access. For initial longer projects, Pro plus measured usage is a reasonable starting point; a subscription is not required merely to test the included workflow.
- Pro and Ultra subscribers can buy one-time AI credit packages through Google One, with examples of 2,500, 5,000 or 20,000 credits in the purchase guide. Check the actual offer and expiry terms in the account.
- Larger regular production may justify Ultra; compare the actual regional price with the observed credits per accepted minute first.

The purchase-specific Google One guide currently says top-ups are available to Pro and Ultra, not Plus, while the Flow overview mentions Plus as well. Treat this as a documentation inconsistency: do not promise Plus top-up eligibility without checking the account. Do not enable auto-reload or buy anything without the user's explicit instruction.
