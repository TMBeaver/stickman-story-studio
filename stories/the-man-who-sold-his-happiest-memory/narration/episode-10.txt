Milo saved his sister. He could not save the jars. By morning, the shop was ash, and the cart stood untouched across the street.

They broke the seals one at a time. Warm lights rose above the rooftops. Across town, people stopped in doorways and remembered their own lives.

The keeper's daughter arrived before noon. He remembered her terrible jokes now. She told another. He laughed before she reached the ending.

The buyer demanded his property. Lena told the officers who had locked them inside. They took him aside. His money couldn't answer their questions.

That evening, Milo opened his sketchbook beside Lena. He tried to draw their father laughing. The face came easily. The afternoon did not.

Then Lena knocked over her lemonade. The drawing was ruined. They looked at each other, and laughed. This time, Milo wasn't missing the moment.
