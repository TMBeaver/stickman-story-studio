The buyer locked them inside with the memories. He had seen the cracked jar. Now he wanted his shipment gone before they broke another.

Lena found a narrow service passage behind the shelves. At its end was the delivery hatch, wide enough for one person at a time.

They had a way out. Two jars. A clear street. Milo could go home with everything he had come to save that night.

But on the shelf beside him was a jar tagged with a child's drawing. A little girl holding the hand of an older man.

He put his jars in his satchel. Then he pulled an empty delivery cart toward the shelves. Lena watched him, sighed, and started loading.

A hand caught the cart before it rolled. The keeper stood in the passage. He looked at the girl's drawing and whispered, My daughter.
