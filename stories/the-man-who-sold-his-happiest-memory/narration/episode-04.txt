A stranger was enjoying Milo's last afternoon with Dad. For the first time that night, being broke did not feel like the worst part.

The buyer called it charming. He asked whether the father had more memories available. The keeper said nothing. Milo nearly stepped out of hiding.

Then the lens shut. The afternoon returned to its jar. Milo watched the keeper remove it and lock it inside a padded case.

Lena had seen the key. She had also seen the delivery door. They could steal two jars, slip outside, and disappear before midnight.

They waited for the keeper to follow the buyer. Then Lena lifted the key while Milo opened the case with both hands shaking.

His sleeve caught a tiny jar on the shelf. It fell. The seal cracked. Somewhere outside, a woman began to cry with relief.
