The falling shelf tore open the furnace chute. Hot glass spilled across the floor. A curtain caught fire. The passage filled with smoke.

Outside, the keeper dragged the memory cart clear. Inside, Lena's coat caught beneath the fallen frame. Milo heard her call his name.

He pulled at the shelf. It would not move. He dropped his satchel, planted both feet, and lifted while Lena pulled herself free.

They stumbled into the rain. Only then did Milo see his bag inside, beside the fire. His last afternoon with Dad was still in it.

He took one step toward the doorway. Lena reached for him. For a second, he held the hand that was here, and wanted what wasn't.

Milo stayed. The ceiling gave way. Behind the falling sparks, two small lights went dark. His father's beginning. Their last afternoon. Both gone.
